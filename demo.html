Interactive demo

Press Space or click the desktop to focus it. Then press Space for grid mode or Shift Space for mouse mode, or hold m for mouse mode until you release it.

This demo is built for desktop. It needs a physical keyboard and a larger screen, so open it on a computer to try it.

Exercises

  • Left-click a target in grid modeBlue rings. Type the cell's label, then press s.
  • Refine a selected cellAfter typing a label, press Return to split the cell into a finer grid.
  • Click the smallest kind of targetA cell is wider than these rings, so refine with Return to aim, then click.
  • Right-click and double-clickHit a pink ring with f and a green ring with c. Either order works.
  • Drag a file into Archive in mouse modeMove onto a file, hold s, move into Archive, release.
  • Scroll the reader with the keyboardIn mouse mode, point at the reader and press u e y o.
